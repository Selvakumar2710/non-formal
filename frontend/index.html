Install Care Clinic on iOS

Install Care Clinic on your iPhone or iPad for fast full-screen access and offline support:

  1. 1
    Tap Share

    In Safari, tap the Share ⎋ button at the bottom (or top on iPad).

  2. 2
    Select "Add to Home Screen"

    Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen ⊞.

  3. 3
    Confirm & Launch

    Tap Add in the top-right corner. The Care Clinic icon will appear on your home screen!
